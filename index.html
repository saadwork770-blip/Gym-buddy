What's inside

Everything from your training plan, made usable

Day-by-day program

The full Upper/Lower x2 split laid out by day with sets, reps, cardio finishers, and every guideline from the original plan — warm-up, rest times, tempo, and progression rules.

Illustrated exercise library

Every machine, cable, dumbbell and cardio move in the plan, with an original diagram and clear step-by-step setup and execution instructions — filterable by day or muscle group.

Local profile & progress

Create a profile, check off exercises as you complete them, and log bodyweight over time to see your trend — stored privately in your own browser.

About this site. Content is sourced directly from a real training plan written for a "Fitness Time" standard commercial gym setting. Exercise diagrams are original line-art illustrations drawn for this project (not photographs), so the site loads instantly and works fully offline. Profiles are stored only in your browser's local storage — there is no login server, so nothing is shared between devices or sent anywhere. This is general training information, not personalized medical or professional coaching advice.
